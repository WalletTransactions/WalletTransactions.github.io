# Security and privacy

!!! warning "Draft"
    This page describes principles; it is not a legal privacy policy. The
    policy required by Google Play has to be a separate page.

- **Your data on your device.** The app works with a local database; the
  server is for sync and for the features that need it.
- **Sign in with Google**, no separate password. Tokens on the phone are kept
  in encrypted Android storage.
- **App lock** — PIN code or fingerprint.
- **Active sessions** are listed in the settings; any of them can be ended.
- **AI never writes to your wallet** and never receives your passes. A local
  model is used by default; a cloud one only with explicit consent.
- **Service logs never contain** your texts or money data.
- **MCP is read-only**, and the token can be revoked at any time.
- **Account deletion** removes all wallet data and the account itself.
