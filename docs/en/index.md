# Count & Save

**Personal finance that works offline, understands plain language and bank
statements, and is equally at hand on your phone, in the browser and in
Telegram.**

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- :material-cellphone: **Android app**

    Full-featured bookkeeping right on your phone — even on a plane. Data
    lives on the device and syncs when the network is back.

- :material-monitor-dashboard: **Web dashboard**

    Analytics on a big screen: spending structure, trends, wallet balances,
    exchange rates and access management.

- :material-send: **Telegram bot**

    "Coffee 200" — and the expense is recorded. Balances, recent operations
    and questions about your money without opening the app.

- :material-robot-happy: **AI assistant**

    Describe expenses in your own words, upload a bank statement — the AI
    parses it and sorts it into categories. You always make the final call.

</div>

## Who it is for

- People with **money in several currencies and banks** — wallets in any
  currency, transfers with conversion and fees, totals in a single currency
  at the rate of the day.
- **Families and couples** — a shared wallet by invitation, each member with
  their own role.
- Anyone **too busy to type in every expense** — enter a phrase, use voice,
  "Share" from another app, or import a whole statement.
- Those who **value privacy** — your own server, a local language model, and
  an AI that cannot change anything in your data.

## The essentials in a minute

| | |
|---|---|
| **Offline first** | The phone keeps the whole database and works without a network; changes go to the server in batches and reach your other devices |
| **Multi-currency** | Any currency, custom currency pairs, rate history, rates from the ECB, the Bank of Russia and Yahoo Finance |
| **One account everywhere** | Sign in with Google; the same data in the app, on the web and in the bot |
| **AI only suggests** | Parsed expenses and statements come back as drafts; only what you confirm is saved |
| **Answers questions** | "How much did I spend on food in September?" — ask the bot or your own AI assistant; the database does the maths, not the model |
