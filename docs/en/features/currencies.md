# Currencies and rates

- **Any currency** — fiat, crypto, your own units. Each has a precision (how
  many decimal places to show).
- **Currency pairs** — a shared directory plus your own pairs.
- **Rate history** with a chart; the rate used is the latest known on the
  required date.
- **Rate import** from open sources: Frankfurter (ECB), the Bank of Russia,
  Yahoo Finance. Manual entry and editing on the phone and on the web.
- **Equivalent** — every report can be shown in one chosen currency. The
  "Show in equivalent" flag keeps only the currencies you need in the picker.
- **Honest totals** — if a rate is missing for conversion, the total is
  marked as incomplete instead of being guessed.
