# Reports and analytics

Full analytics lives in the web dashboard; brief reports are in the app and
the bot. One filter is shared by all screens: **period**, **grouping** (day,
week, month…) and **equivalent currency**.

## Web dashboard screens

| Screen | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Four key figures for the period, an income and expense chart with a balance line, medians and spread, a mini spending structure, spending by weekday, and "Biggest changes" when comparing periods |
| **Spending structure** | Category shares for the period and how they shifted versus the previous period |
| **Trends** | Income and expense by interval with a breakdown of the selected interval |
| **Categories over time** | The trend of a single category |
| **Wallets and balances** | Each wallet's balance at the start and end of the period, turnover, a 12-month sparkline, breakdown by currency |
| **Operations** | A day-by-day feed with search, filters by type, item and wallet, totals for the filter and top items |
| **Exchange rates** | Pairs and rate history with manual editing |
| **Icons** | Your icons and the shared set |

## In the app

Income and expenses by category for a period, wallet balances, rate chart.

## In Telegram and in AI assistants

Balances, recent operations and answers to questions like "how much went on
taxis this month" or "compare summer and winter food spending" — see
[Telegram bot](telegram.md) and [MCP](mcp.md).
